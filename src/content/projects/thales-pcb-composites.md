## Context & goal

Printed circuit boards in spacecraft rely on glass/epoxy composites whose properties drift as they age. In this industrial innovation project with Thales Alenia Space, we characterise the microstructure of aged PCB composites and identify their damage mechanisms.

## Method

**Done so far.**

- Literature review: cores, prepregs, cure states of the resin (A, B and C stages) and ageing.
- Inventory of the characterisation methods available and relevant.
- A microscopy plan.

**Digital microscopy plan** (Keyence VHX, ×20 to ×6000), built on five indicators:

1. Colour
2. Surface cracks
3. Surface relief
4. Thickness of the oxidised layer
5. Internal damage, on cross-sections
